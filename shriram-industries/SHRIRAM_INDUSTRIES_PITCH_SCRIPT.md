## 2. Exact Phone Call Script (Hindi / Hinglish)

> *"Namaste Uncle ji, Pranaam! Main Suryaansh bol raha hoon... [Papa's Name] ji ka beta."*

#### **Step 2: Reference Your Recent Visit**

#### **Step 3: The Observation & The Live Gift**
> *"Toh main software aur web technology mein kaam karta hoon. Maine notice kiya ki aaj kal bade architects, interior designers aur builders pehle phone pe search karte hain.*  
>  
> *Aapka jo itna uncha level ka hardware aur kitchen baskets hain — AISI 304 quality — waisa koi digital showroom online nahi tha jo aapke real status ko reflect kare.*  
>  
> *Toh maine socha sirf bolne se achha hai main aapke liye kuch banake dikhaun. Over the weekend maine specially Shriram Industries ke liye ek complete live website ready kari hai."*

#### **Step 4: The WhatsApp Link Drop (Low Pressure)**

#### **Step 5: The In-Person Close**
> *"Aap free hoke dekh lijiye Uncle, fir main ek din Polo Ground factory aake 10 minute aapse milta hoon chai pe, agar aapko pasand aaye toh batana."*

### ⏱️ Delivery: 3 to 5 Days
* **Rule:** Never say *"It's already ready, I can launch in 1 hour."* That makes him think it took no effort.
* **Say this:**  
  > *"Uncle, prototype maine live dikha diya hai. Lekin aapka official domain connect karne, exact technical specs confirm karne aur mobile testing mein 3 se 4 working days ka time lagega."*

### 💳 Payment Terms: 50 / 50 Milestone Split
* **50% (₹5,000) Advance Token:** Paid when he gives the green light to initiate DNS mapping and final catalog touches.
* **50% (₹5,000) + Maintenance (₹5,000) = ₹10,000 Final:** Paid on the day the website is live on his official domain and approved.

## 6. Objection Handling for Uncle

### Q1: *"Beta humare paas domain already hai purana."*
> **Answer:**  
> *"Bohot badhiya Uncle! Iska matlab aapko online presence ki value pehle se pata hai. Domain toh bas dukan ka name-board hota hai. Hum bina kisi extra jhanjhat ke aapke usi existing domain ke sath is modern site ko 1 ghante mein connect kar denge."*

### Q2: *"Hum B2B manufacturer hain, hume website se direct customer thodi chahiye?"*
> **Answer:**  
> *"Uncle, bilkul sahi baat hai. Ye website retail customers ke liye nahi, balki bade **dealers, modular kitchen fabricators aur architects** ke liye hai. Jab MP ya bahar ka koi naya dealer aapke baare mein sunta hai, toh wo sabse pehle phone pe search karta hai. Jab wo aapki technical specs aur SS304 testing certificates dekhega, toh uska trust 10x badh jaayega."*

### Q3: *"Maintenance ka saal ka ₹5,000 kyu du?"*
> **Answer:**  
> *"Uncle, ₹5,000 saal ka matlab lagbhag ₹400 per month (roz ka ₹13). Isme high-speed cloud server, SSL security padlock, aur saal bhar aapka digital management included hai. Dukaan pe koi naya model aaye ya rate change ho, aapko bas photo WhatsApp karni hai aur main update kar dunga."*
